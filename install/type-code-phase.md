# Phase

A phase is an emergent chunk of a Mission — a milestone-sized slice of the goal, containing multiple coherent groups of work rather than a single change — created as a derivative of its parent mission with its own short-term memory, state, and iteration journal. Phases are not planned in full before a mission starts; their shape is discovered as the work unfolds, which is why a mission creates new phases on demand to adapt. Phases exist so a long-running mission does not collapse into one unmanageable file: each phase resumes cold from its own short-term memory and journal, runs the mission's loop over its slice of the goal across many cycles, and records the commits it produced. Reach for a Phase as the working chunk inside a Mission; reach for a Task when the unit of work is bounded enough to stand on its own outside a mission.
